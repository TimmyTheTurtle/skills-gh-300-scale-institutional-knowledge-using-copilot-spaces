# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects through an iterative lifecycle focused on customer value, clear ownership, and measurable outcomes. Teams initiate work by validating the need, identifying stakeholders and success measures, and making a go/no-go decision. They then plan scope, prioritize and estimate the backlog, map dependencies and risks, and agree on milestones.

During execution, teams deliver in small increments using the project board and pull request workflow, test and review changes, monitor progress and outcome metrics, and escalate blockers as needed. Pre-release checks cover acceptance criteria, CI and security results, release notes, and rollback plans; after deployment, teams verify the release and roll back if necessary. Teams close the loop through retrospectives, capturing learnings and tracking improvement actions with owners and due dates.
